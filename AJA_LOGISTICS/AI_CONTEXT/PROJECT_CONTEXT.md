# PROJECT CONTEXT - AJA LOGISTICS

## Overview
Aja Logistics Company is an authorized logistics provider in Saudi Arabia and the GCC region. The platform serves cargo owners, importers, exporters, and logistics operators with end-to-end freight visibility.

## Core Services
1. **Sea Freight (الشحن البحري الدولي)**: Full Container Load (FCL) & Less than Container Load (LCL).
2. **Land Freight (الشحن البري المحلي والخليجي)**: Heavy cargo, curtain-side, and temperature-controlled trailers across KSA and GCC.
3. **Customs Clearance (التخليص الجمركي الفوري)**: Express processing integrated with Fasah platform across Saudi sea ports and land borders.
4. **Warehousing & Storage (التخزين والمستودعات)**: Air-conditioned storage with WMS inventory management in Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam.

## Target User Roles
- **Customer (العميل)**: Tracks shipments, requests quotes, manages compliance documents, and talks with Aja AI Assistant.
- **Operations Staff (فريق العمليات)**: Updates shipment milestones, processes quote requests, verifies documents, and handles live chat.
- **Admin (مدير النظام)**: Full system governance, audit logs, CMS management, and RBAC user permissions.
