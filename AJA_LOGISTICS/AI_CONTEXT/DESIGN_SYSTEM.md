# DESIGN SYSTEM - AJA LOGISTICS

## DESIGN DIRECTION
Premium Global Logistics Technology.

## VISUAL LANGUAGE
- **Deep**: Rich dark navy tones paired with clean white and metallic grays.
- **Clean**: Spacious padding, high contrast typography, clutter-free layouts.
- **Precise**: Exact grid alignments, mathematical padding scales, no random values.
- **Structured**: Modular card systems, clear data tables, predictable component rhythm.
- **Cinematic**: Controlled lighting, elegant subtle depth, smooth transitions.

## COLOR SYSTEM
- **Primary**: Deep Navy (`#0F2C59`)
- **Secondary**: Graphite (`#1E293B` / `#334155`)
- **Neutrals**: White (`#FFFFFF`), Light Gray (`#F8FAFC` / `#F1F5F9`), Metallic Gray (`#94A3B8` / `#64748B`)
- **Single Accent Color**: Warm Amber / Gold (`#E5A93C` / `#F59E0B`)
  - Used exclusively for: **Routes**, **Active States**, **CTA Buttons**, **Network Nodes**
  - *Strict Rule*: No secondary competing accent colors.

## TYPOGRAPHY
- **Arabic**: Modern, clean, and highly readable Arabic typeface (Tajawal / IBM Plex Sans Arabic / Plus Jakarta Sans).
- **English**: Modern geometric typeface (Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter).
- **Priorities**: Readability, Hierarchy, Premium Appearance.

## SPACING
- Strict 8-point spatial system (4px, 8px, 12px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 64px).
- Zero arbitrary or random padding/margin values.

## COMPONENT STYLES
- **Cards**: Clean, structured, subtle depth (`border-slate-200/80 shadow-sm rounded-2xl bg-white`).
- **Buttons**: Clear, strong, high contrast (`#0F2C59` primary, `#E5A93C` secondary/CTA).
- **Navigation**: Minimalist, premium, easy to scan with clear active indicators.

## MOTION
- **Intentional, Smooth, Controlled**: Subtle fade transitions, micro-hover elevation, route entry motion. No gratuitous animation.
