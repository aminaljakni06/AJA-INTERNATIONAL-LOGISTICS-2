# PRODUCT VISION - AJA LOGISTICS

## Mission
To empower trade across Saudi Arabia and the GCC with smart, transparent, digital-first logistics and express customs clearance.

## Key Value Propositions
- **Real-Time Visibility**: Live stage-by-stage shipment tracking with timeline events and instant notification alerts.
- **Instant AI Assistance**: Powered by Gemini AI (musa'id Aja / مساعد أجا الذكي) to answer shipping inquiries, document requirements, and status checks.
- **Unified Portal**: Seamless experience bridging public marketing, client self-service portal, and operational admin management.
