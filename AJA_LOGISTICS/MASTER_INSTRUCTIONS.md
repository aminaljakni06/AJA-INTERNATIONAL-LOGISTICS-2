# AJA LOGISTICS - MASTER INSTRUCTIONS & DEVELOPMENT GOVERNANCE

## Project Overview
This repository contains the official codebase and specification system for **Aja Logistics Company (شركة أجا للخدمات اللوجستية)** — a premier Saudi and GCC digital logistics, sea & land freight, and customs clearance platform.

## Principles of Development
1. **Brand Integrity**: All representations must reflect "Aja Logistics" (شركة أجا للخدمات اللوجستية) with strict alignment to official navy `#0F2C59` and gold/amber `#E5A93C` brand tokens.
2. **Bilingual Excellence**: Native support for Arabic (`ar`, RTL) as primary and English (`en`, LTR) as secondary, maintained dynamically via `LanguageContext`.
3. **Enterprise Security**: Complete server-side proxying for sensitive keys, Firestore security rules enforcement, and RBAC (Customer, Operations Staff, Admin).
4. **Performance & Quality**: Fast build compilation, zero linter warnings, accessible UI, and mobile-first responsive design.
