# PAGE STRUCTURE - AJA LOGISTICS

## Public Pages
- `HomePage`: Hero search, core services, network coverage, benefits, CTA.
- `SeaFreightPage`, `LandFreightPage`, `CustomsPage`, `WarehousingPage`: Dedicated service offerings.
- `TrackingPage`: Interactive shipment timeline and map simulation.
- `QuoteRequestPage`: Form wizard with instant request generation.
- `DownloadAppPage`: Mobile app download links (iOS & Android).
- `LoginPage` & `RegisterPage`: Authentication and password reset wizard.

## Customer Portal Pages
- `CustomerDashboard`: Overview metrics, active shipments, quick actions.
- `CustomerShipments`: Grid/Table view of all customer orders.
- `CustomerQuotes`: Status of submitted quote requests.
- `CustomerDocuments`: Electronic bill of lading, customs declarations, invoices.
- `CustomerAIAssistant`: Interactive Gemini AI chat widget.

## Admin Operational Pages
- `AdminDashboard`: Fleet status, revenue summary, active operations.
- `AdminShipments`: Full shipment manager with status update triggers.
- `AdminQuotes`: Quote pricing calculator and proposal sender.
- `AdminCMS`: Content management for announcements and FAQs.
- `AdminAudit`: Security audit logs.
