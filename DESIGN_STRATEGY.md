## 6. ACCESSIBILITY & INCLUSIVITY STANDARDS (WCAG 2.2 AA)

- **Contrast Ratios**: Minimum 4.5:1 for body text, 3.0:1 for large display headers and UI controls.
- **Focus States**: High-visibility 2px solid focus rings (`#1F4E79` / `#2F3437`) across all interactive controls.
- **Touch Targets**: Minimum 44x44px target sizes on mobile and touch devices.
- **Screen Reader Support**: Full ARIA labels, semantic landmark markup (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<nav>`, `<footer>`, `<section>`).
- **Color Independence**: Statuses and telemetry indicators never rely solely on color; text labels and distinct geometric iconography accompany every state.

---

## 7. BILINGUAL & RTL / LTR ARCHITECTURAL HARMONY

- **Symmetric Balance**: Equal visual weight in both Arabic (RTL) and English (LTR).
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - **Arabic**: *IBM Plex Sans Arabic* / *Cairo* (structured, crisp, legibility-first typography).
  - **English**: *Plus Jakarta Sans* / *Inter* (modern, geometric, enterprise-grade sans-serif).
- **Directional Mirroring**: Automatic SVG arrow flipping (`rotate-180 rtl:rotate-0`), logical margin/padding utilities (`ms-`, `me-`, `ps-`, `pe-`).

---

## 8. RESPONSIVE SYSTEM & MULTI-DEVICE STRATEGY

- **Fluid Breakpoints**:
  - `sm`: 640px (Mobile Landscape / Large Phones)
  - `md`: 768px (Tablets / Medium Screens)
  - `lg`: 1024px (Laptops / Small Desktops)
  - `xl`: 1280px (Enterprise Workstations)
  - `2xl`: 1536px (Ultra-wide Monitors - constrained with `max-w-7xl mx-auto`)
- **Adaptation Philosophy**: Reorganize and reflow structural cards rather than hiding essential operational content.

---

## 9. ICONOGRAPHY & GRAPHIC SYSTEM RULES

- **Style**: Strict line-art geometry, 1.5px to 2px uniform stroke weight, zero filled or 3D cartoon icons.
- **Domain Metaphors**: Anchors, Cargo Vessels, Aircraft, Transport Fleets, Warehouses, Barcodes, Route Nodes, Customs Seals.
- **Provider**: Standardized `lucide-react` vector set across all components.

---

## 10. MOTION & INTERACTION PRINCIPLES

- **Purposeful & Subdued**: Motion provides context, spatial orientation, and state confirmation.
- **Duration & Easing**: Fast, responsive transitions (150ms–300ms) with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
- **Zero Distraction**: No looping animations or decorative floating elements that impede data scanning.
